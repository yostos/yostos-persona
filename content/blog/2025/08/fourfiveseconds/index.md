Guitar: Epiphon USA Texan, Epiphone Casino

This hit features Paul on acoustic guitar. My Texan just turned one year old, so I was looking for songs to play with it when this one came to mind.

Unlike most Japanese songs, this track expresses raw emotion without offering resolution or moral lessons. I've been dealing with a lot of frustration lately, and playing this made me realize the value of music that simply holds your feelings without demanding answers. Of course, I personally have a 2-second fuse anyway 😁.
