A wall of glass and steel rises into the sky, grid upon grid, clear yet impenetrable. You can see through it, but you can never reach what's on the other side. Sometimes the most effective fences are the ones you don't even notice.

This is Abeno Harukas in Osaka, Japan's tallest skyscraper at 300 meters. Completed in 2014, it houses a department store, offices, a hotel, a museum, and an observation deck. The name 'Harukas' comes from an archaic Japanese word meaning 'to brighten' or 'to clear up.' A building named after clarity, wrapped entirely in a fence you can see right through.
